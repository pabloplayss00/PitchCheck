Teams and competitions are real: rosters and 2026-27 promotions/relegations for the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1 are current; attack/defence ratings are derived from each club's real 2025-26 goals-for/against per game (newly promoted sides use a standard "promoted side" baseline until they build a track record).

League fixtures and results are real, snapshotted from footballwebpages.co.uk on 3 September 2026 — see data.js and the README to refresh them. Champions League and Europa League here are drawn only from clubs in those five leagues by final league position, with an approximate cross-league strength adjustment — not the full real draw. Domestic cup pairings shown are illustrative: real draws for these rounds haven't been made yet this early in the season.

Player Watch uses each club's real current squad; referees are real, currently-active officials with a sourced cards-per-game figure. In both cases the odds shown are this model's estimate, not a licensed per-player or per-official statistic — see a match page's footer note for detail.
