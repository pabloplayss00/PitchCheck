                Teams and competitions are real: rosters and 2026-27 promotions/relegations for the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1 are current; attack/defence ratings start from each club's real 2025-26 goals-for/against per game (newly promoted sides use a standard "promoted side" baseline until they build a track record), then blend in that club's actual goals for/against over its last 6 played matches this season, so ratings move with current form rather than staying fixed all year.

                League fixtures and results are real, snapshotted from footballwebpages.co.uk on 3 September 2026 — see data.js and the README to refresh them. Champions League and Europa League here are drawn only from clubs in those five leagues by final league position, with an approximate cross-league strength adjustment — not the full real draw. Domestic cup pairings shown are illustrative: real draws for these rounds haven't been made yet this early in the season.

                Player Watch uses each club's real current squad; referees are real, currently-active officials with a sourced cards-per-game figure. In both cases the odds shown are this model's estimate — a team or referee total split across real names by position, prominence and disciplinary tendency — not a licensed per-player or per-official statistic, and squads/appointments will drift out of date as transfers and rotations happen (see the README to refresh ROSTERS and REFEREES_BY_LEAGUE in data.js). Shots on target, corners, fouls and saves are modelled from team-level style proxies the same way, not official match-report figures.
